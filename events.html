What we host

Small, mostly. Which is the good part.

Criollo is not a banquet hall, and that is exactly why it works. Tapas service means the food keeps coming, nobody waits on a plated entrée, and the room stays loud in the right way.

01

Rehearsal dinners

Family in from out of town, a long table, plates landing every few minutes. We have run these alongside the wedding party the following night.

02

Wedding receptions

Intimate receptions in the dining room and out on the patio — including one recent evening for fifty guests.

03

Birthdays & holidays

Annual Christmas dinners, milestone birthdays, and groups of friends who want small plates and a bartender paying attention.

04

Company & club nights

Buy-outs, wine-led dinners and after-hours gatherings. Live music can be arranged — we have hosted musicians before.

The spaces

Dining room, bar, patio — or all three.

The dining room seats a long table under the horse relief. The bar handles a standing crowd. The patio has a fireplace and, on the right night, a s'mores station.

Menus are built to your party — a set list of pintxos and tasting plates, passed or family style, with cocktails and wine to match. Tell us the headcount and the mood and we'll come back with a plan.

Availability, minimums and buy-out pricing depend on the night and the season. Send the form below or call and we'll walk you through it.
